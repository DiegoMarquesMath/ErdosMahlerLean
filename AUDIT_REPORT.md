**Adversarial checks of the sensitive estimates.**

For the small-denominator count, I recomputed the three shell terms independently:

\[
Q^{-4}\left(NV^{-7}+N^{\kappa/(\kappa+1)}V^{1234/5117}+V^2\right).
\]

The decreasing term is controlled at `Y=Q³/6`; the two increasing terms are controlled at the final lower dyadic endpoint, which is at most `H=Q⁹/t`. Thus the last shell may extend past `H` without invalidating the summation bound. The middle numerator exponent is `85212/5117<17`, so after dividing by `Q⁴` it is at most `Q¹³≤B/t²`, using `t²≤Q`. The last term is exactly `B/t²`. These inequalities rule out a missed factor of `Q⁴` or a divergent terminal shell. The passage to arbitrary epsilon uses a threshold depending only on epsilon and the fixed counting constant.

For the Matveev gap, I checked the sign-sensitive step: for `q≥1`, `tau ↦ tau(1+log(max(q,a/tau)))` is nondecreasing for positive tau, across both branches of the maximum. This permits the upper height bound `tau≤11w/q`; replacing tau while leaving an inappropriate unweighted coefficient bound would not justify the proof. Assuming the gap fails gives `delta≤w exp(gamma q)`, with `gamma=1/(4·10^12)`. The coefficient maximum is bounded by `q exp(gamma q)`. The strict condition `10^12(1+log q)/q<1/4` gives a lower bound above `−11w/2`, contradicting the upper logarithmic error `log 2−11w` for `w≥1`. Nonvanishing is established before taking the logarithm. Zero coefficients and dependent bases are handled by the actual external corollary, not by an independence assumption.

For the rank split, rank zero and diagonal pairs were retained. In the low-rank case `d≤x^(2/3)`, `q≤Kx` and an internally proved eventual logarithmic estimate give `q^d≤e^(x/4)`. In the high-rank case, `x>0` ensures positive rank, and `M d²≤456B` gives `M/B≤456x^(-4/3)`. The finite-grid sums leave terms bounded by constants times

\[
\chi^{-1/3}+e^{-3\chi/4}
+Q^{4-18/\kappa}e^{-(\beta-1/4)\chi}+Q^{-19/2}.
\]

The extra `chi^(-4/3)` in the explicit Lean bound is absorbed once `chi≥1`. The signs are correct: `18−4 kappa=383/250>0` and `beta−1/4=8351/16468>0`. The proof constructs independent sufficiently large coordinate and stage thresholds and then combines them; it does not interchange `forall t` and `exists constant`. No bound on the number of selected blocks is assumed.

For coverage, I checked denominators equal to the small/large cutoff, equal to an interior block boundary, and in the final truncated interval. A denominator equal to `Q⁹/t` belongs to the small range. An interior endpoint belongs to the preceding block because intervals are `(V_j,H_j]`. The least-index proof in `BlockCoverage.lean:22–38` guards its predecessor subtraction. `16v^10<3^b`, together with `b<Q+N`, proves `v<F`; the final cap is not silently omitted. A return can have witnesses in several blocks, but the proof uses a union upper bound, so it never needs those sets of returns to be disjoint.
